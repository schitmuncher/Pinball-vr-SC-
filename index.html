2 · Play

No VR headset found here. Open this page in the Meta Quest browser to play in VR.

In VR: triggers or grips flip, hold A or X to pull the plunger and let go to launch, flick the right stick to nudge. B pauses, Y starts a new game. Click the left stick to move the table in front of you, push it up or down to change the height. With hand tracking, pinch to flip and pinch with both hands to launch.

Z / slash: flippers · Space: plunger · X . ↑: nudge · F2: new game · P: pause · drag to look around
